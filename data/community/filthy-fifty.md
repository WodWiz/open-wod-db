# Filthy Fifty

A ten-movement chipper with 50 reps of each: box jumps, jumping pull-ups, kettlebell swings, walking lunge steps, knees-to-elbows, push presses, back extensions, wall-ball shots, burpees and double-unders.

**For Time**
  50 Box Jump (24")
  50 Jumping Pull Up
  50 Kettlebell Swing (1 pood)
  50 Walking Lunge
  50 Knees To Elbows
  50 Push Press (45lb)
  50 Back Extension
  50 Wall Ball Shot (20lb)
  50 Burpee
  50 Double Under
