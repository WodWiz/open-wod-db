# Fight Gone Bad

Three five-minute rounds of five stations worked for a minute each: wall-ball shots, sumo deadlift high pulls, box jumps, push presses and rowing. One point per rep (or calorie on the rower), with a minute of rest between rounds.

**3 rounds of 60 s work, 60 s rest**
  Wall Ball Shot (20lb)
  Sumo Deadlift High Pull (75lb)
  Box Jump (20")
  Push Press (75lb)
  Row

*Type: Interval · Category: Community*
