# Bear Complex

Five rounds of a barbell complex of power clean, front squat, push press, back squat and push press, done as 7 unbroken sets per round. Scored by the load used.

**Complex, 5 rounds**
  1 Power Clean
  1 Front Squat
  1 Push Press
  1 Back Squat
  1 Push Press
