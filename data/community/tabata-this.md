# Tabata This

Five Tabata intervals of rowing, air squats, pull-ups, push-ups and sit-ups, with a minute of rest after each of the first four. You score the lowest interval for each movement.

**Tabata: 8 rounds of 20 s on, 10 s off, per movement**
  Row
  Air Squat
  Pull Up
  Push Up
  Sit Up

*Type: Tabata · Category: Community*
