# Tabata Something Else

Four Tabata intervals back to back with no rest between them: pull-ups, push-ups, sit-ups and air squats. The score is the total reps across all 32 work intervals.

**Tabata: 8 rounds of 20 s on, 10 s off, per movement**
  Pull Up
  Push Up
  Sit Up
  Air Squat

*Type: Tabata · Category: Community*
