# Wood

Five timed rounds of a 400 m run, 10 burpee box jumps, 10 sumo deadlift high pulls and 10 thrusters, with a minute of rest between rounds.

**5 rounds for time**
  Run 400m
  10 Burpee Box Jump (24"/20")
  10 Sumo Deadlift High Pull (95lb/65lb)
  10 Thruster (95lb/65lb)
